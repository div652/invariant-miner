analysts rated confidence high for 25 tasks and medium for 40. Labels are judgments: the I/G/M boundary in particular
is often a close call.

## Findings across tasks
- **Model mistakes are most of it (79% of lost points; 62 of 65 tasks).** Every task was fully attempted, but answers
  are thin: the agent finds most planted issues, then loses all-or-nothing rubric items because one specific number,
  name or step is missing, wrong or generic. Of the model-mistake points, 37% are wrong analysis, 35% facts it had
  but left out, 22% professional checks it did not do, 6% ignored instructions.
- **Retrieval is rarely the problem (2.5%).** The agent reads its inputs in full. The few R items are web documents
  it found but did not open (Schedule C, a worked-examples PDF, ISO text) or searched only through snippets.
- **Insufficient information (8.6%, 19 tasks)**: rubric items that expect specifics the prompt never states: a
  required column or label ("Priority Training Track", `discrepancy_type`, "Critical"), sheet names, thresholds or
  tiers.
- **Grading problems (6.2%, 18 tasks).** With them passed, the pooled score would be 0.373 instead of 0.332:
  - the judge cut 3 large workbooks at its 200K-character limit and failed items on sheets it never saw
    (computer_and_information_systems_managers/task2, petroleum_engineers/task1, social_science_research_assistants/task2);
  - the judge missed content that is there (a chart, cell fills);
  - rubric values that differ from current sources.
- **Formulas without stored values.** This judge reads spreadsheets with pandas, which shows a formula the agent never
  computed as blank. 4 workbooks had such formulas: producers/task1 (137), sales_representatives_..._task1 (99),
  secretaries_..._task1 (2,994 of 8,002) and training_and_development_specialists/task1 (84). The newer upstream
  judge (job-bench-eval 214e635) recalculates them with LibreOffice.
- **Subagents: findings got lost in the hand-off.** In about 11 tasks a subagent found a fact or document the rubric
  wanted, but the root agent did not carry it into the deliverable. In 3 tasks the root followed a subagent's wrong
  advice: data_entry_keyers/task2, secretaries_..._task2 and technical_writers/task2.
