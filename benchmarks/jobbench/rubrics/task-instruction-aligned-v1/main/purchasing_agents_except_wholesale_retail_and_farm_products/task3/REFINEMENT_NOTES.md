## Likely judge artifacts versus genuine task failures

Likely artifacts include requiring exact hidden model names, exact claim identifiers and dates in prose, an exact preselected discount calculation, or a predetermined vendor recommendation. Those facts may support scoring when present in the sources, but equivalent accurate descriptions satisfy the task.

Genuine failures include reversing pass/fail logic, treating missing evidence as a pass, using non-Critical severity for a Mandatory = YES failure, omitting vendor-by-requirement rows, failing to compare PR quantities/specifications, arithmetic errors, missing the required bid-tabulation or scorecard structure, missing scenarios, incorrect score weights, missing history metrics, a non-DOCX memo, incomplete section order, untraceable negotiation math, or the wrong escalation target.
