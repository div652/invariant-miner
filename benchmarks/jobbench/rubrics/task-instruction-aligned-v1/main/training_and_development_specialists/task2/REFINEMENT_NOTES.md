# Refinement notes — training_and_development_specialists/task2

## Instruction-supported requirements

The refined instructions require a four-sheet workbook, a Word evaluation redesign, and a PDF director memo. The workbook must calculate completion, satisfaction, and gain by four groupings; flag module-department combinations below the stated thresholds; reconcile instructors with Q4 availability; schedule every Q3 module while covering each mandatory module for every department under all room and instructor constraints; and calculate external-instructor costs with the stated session-hour rules. The redesign must open with Evaluator Instructions, audit all named instruments and Q3 fields, and provide a Level 1–3 mixed-format instrument with diagnostics tied to Q3 evidence. The memo must answer Priorities 1–5, include at least five quantitative facts and a qualitative theme, provide a likelihood-by-impact matrix, recommend retain/replace/renegotiate for each external consultant, and end with exactly three quick wins.

The instructions do not establish one mandatory Kirkpatrick classification for a mixed instrument when its actual items support more than one level. They also require identity inconsistencies to be documented, not silently resolved by matching a surname.

## Material changes and reasons

1. Q3 Analysis scoring now covers every required grouping, count, threshold flag, module total, and comment theme rather than only two modules.
2. Instructor scoring separates genuine identity matches from credential conflicts and missing availability records.
3. Schedule scoring now checks the full constraint set rather than only mandatory-module coverage.
4. The required budget formulas and subtotals received direct weight.
5. Instrument audits are judged from actual items and fields; a nuanced mixed-level mapping is not penalized for disagreeing with a hidden one-level answer.
6. The exact Evaluator Instructions opening and all required item types are directly scored.
7. Memo scoring includes all five priorities, risk matrix, every external consultant, evidence uncertainty, and exactly three quick wins.

## Likely judge artifacts versus genuine task failures

Likely artifacts include forcing the Smartsheet onboarding form to Level 1 only regardless of its actual items, requiring one exact statement of each instrument's level coverage, and zeroing a bundled row when the audit was present but more nuanced.

Genuine failures include missing groupings or threshold flags, wrong instructor metrics, treating Self-Paced as an instructor, silently merging credential conflicts, room or instructor constraint violations, missing mandatory coverage, continued self-paced use after documented underperformance, incorrect hourly costing, no exact Evaluator Instructions preamble, incomplete instrument audits, generic diagnostics, omitted priorities, missing consultant recommendations, absent risk matrix, fewer or more than exactly three quick wins, or insufficient quantitative and qualitative evidence.
